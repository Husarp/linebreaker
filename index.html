NORMAL MODE

Classic block stacking. Stack pieces to complete horizontal lines — each line cleared earns points and XP. Speed increases over time. Earn XP, level up, and unlock new features.

Clear multiple lines at once for combo bonuses. Higher speed = more XP per game!
FUN MODE

Sandbox — no XP, no ranking. Configure board size (6–16 columns, 15–30 rows) and starting speed (1–15) before each game. Same controls as Normal Mode.

ACHIEVEMENTS LOCKED
Achievements cannot be earned in Fun Mode — except those that explicitly mention Fun Mode in their description.
MISSIONS
DAILY QUESTS
3–5 quests that refresh every day. Milestones at 6 · 9 · 12 pts award Parts.
WEEKLY MISSIONS
5 missions reset every Monday. Milestones at 80 · 100 · 120 · 140 pts. Daily pts count too.

🔥 DAILY STREAK
Complete at least 1 daily quest per day. Streak XP bonuses: ×1.25 @ 5 days · ×1.50 @ 10 · ×1.75 @ 20 · ×2.00 @ 30.
CHANGE QUEST
Each non-secret quest can be swapped once. Daily and weekly Change buttons are independent. Replacement is the same tier.
Daily quests and weekly missions cannot be completed in Fun Mode.
GLOSSARY
Single
Clearing 1 line at once.
Double
Clearing 2 lines at once with a single piece placement.
Triple
Clearing 3 lines at once with a single piece placement.
Quad
Clearing 4 lines at once — the maximum possible in one placement. Highest score per drop.
Hard Drop
Press Space — the piece instantly falls to the lowest valid row and locks. Earns bonus score.
Soft Drop
Hold ↓ / S — the piece falls faster while the key is held. Small score bonus per row.
T-Spin
Rotating the T-piece into a tight gap so 3 of its 4 corners are blocked. Clears lines with a bonus score multiplier.
Combo
A streak of consecutive drops where each one clears at least one line. Each extra step in the streak adds a score bonus.
Speed
The pace at which pieces fall. Increases automatically over time. Higher speed = more XP per game and unlocks harder missions.
